01 · SURFACE / ABOUT
Engineering profile

Background & approach.

Civil engineer from Egypt with a B.Sc. in Civil Engineering from Suez Canal University. My work spans land development, wet utilities, stormwater and roads on projects in the United States, New Zealand and Egypt.

Day to day I model in Civil 3D (surfaces, feature lines, pipe and pressure networks, duct banks, COGO points) and turn the model into plan sets, drainage reports and review responses.

Every grade, pipe and note on the sheet has a code reference behind it.
