// 01 · ABOUTEngineering profile

Background & approach

I'm a civil engineer from Egypt with a B.Sc. in Civil Engineering from Suez Canal University. My work spans land development, wet utilities, stormwater and road infrastructure on projects in the United States, New Zealand and Egypt.

Day to day I model in Civil 3D (pipe networks, pressure networks, duct banks, feature lines and COGO points) and turn that model into permit-ready plan sets, drainage reports and review responses.

Every grade, pipe and note on the sheet has a code reference behind it.
